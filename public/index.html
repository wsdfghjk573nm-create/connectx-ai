Messaging that respects your time — and your privacy.

Real-time chats, groups & communities, 1:1 voice and video calls, polls, scheduled and disappearing messages — plus a built-in AI assistant that never reads your private conversations.

⚡ Real-time everything

Instant delivery, typing indicators, read receipts — synced across your devices.

👥 Groups & Communities

Admin-controlled groups, invite links, temporary groups, multi-channel communities and broadcast channels.

📞 Voice & video calls

1-to-1 WebRTC calls with screen sharing where your browser supports it.

🤖 AI assistant, on your terms

Summarize a chat only when you tap the button. Writing help and translations run locally by default.

⏰ Smart messaging

Schedule messages for later, set disappearing-message timers, pin, star, react and search everything.
